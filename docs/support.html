First launch on your Mac

This free preview targets Apple Silicon (ARM64) and macOS 14 or later. It has been tested on Apple Silicon with macOS 27.2. It is ad hoc signed and unnotarized, distributed outside the Mac App Store; macOS may block the first launch.

  1. Get the ZIP or DMG from the official release page, compare its SHA-256 checksum, and move Karim Ink.app into Applications.
  2. If an unidentified-developer warning appears, check that you trust the download. After trying to open it, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, choose Open Anyway for Karim Ink where available, and confirm Open.

This is a per-app exception described in Apple’s first-launch guidance. Do not disable Gatekeeper or remove quarantine flags. If the alert says the app contains malware or is damaged, stop and report the exact message. A managed school/work Mac may not allow an exception.

Quick setup checks

  • Download will not open: follow the first-launch guide above for an unidentified-developer warning. Keep macOS security protections enabled and report the exact message for other warnings.
  • Missing pressure or tilt: check your tablet's macOS driver, its application profile and relevant driver permissions. The app uses standard AppKit pen events and cannot create data the driver does not provide.
  • AI will not reply: local chat needs a ready Apple model. OpenAI needs internet, your own API key and separate API credit. Check the displayed route and error.
  • Recognition is wrong: DeTeXt recognizes one symbol at a time; OCR and spelling can misread engineering notation. Check numbers, units and symbols before changing the drawing.
  • Craft or Notion differs from the Mac app: Craft updating is one-way for supported basic objects. Notion uses an exported web companion. Neither embeds the native engine or offers shared editing.
