# SketchGlide icon

Original vector artwork created for SketchGlide on 12 October 2026: a continuous cream S curve on a teal tile, with a gold nib and dot. No external logo or SF Symbol is used. The included AppKit generator `BuildSupport/RenderMark.swift` renders the mark. Native icon sizes were produced with sips and iconutil. Copyright 2026 Karim Eldamaty; MIT licensed.
