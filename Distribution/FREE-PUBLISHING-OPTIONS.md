# Free publishing and discovery options for SketchGlide

Made by Karim Eldamaty. Checked 12 October 2026. This is a practical selection of useful routes, not every website on the internet. Free standard routes do not include paid advertising, priority reviews or optional services.

The standard routes below have **no mandatory publishing/listing fee**. Account checks, quotas and review still apply. Download payments, signing certificates and optional cloud AI can have separate costs. [Copy-ready announcement drafts](Launch-Copy/README.md) are prepared for DEV, Product Hunt, AlternativeTo, Show HN, LinkedIn and finished video uploads.

Download hosts, app directories, community announcements and search tools serve different purposes. A prepared submission is not an approved public listing.

## Additional candidate: MacUpdate

Its standard submission guide accepts a Mac download URL and describes review. No listing fee is confirmed on that public guide; check the account flow before treating it as a free route. [Submission guide](https://www.macupdate.com/help/submit-app).

## App Store costs

Microsoft Store: new-flow developer registration has no fee. Start at storedeveloper.microsoft.com; old entry points can show the legacy registration flow. A valid identity and Store package are still needed. [Microsoft account instructions](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/apps/publish/partner-center/open-a-developer-account?tabs=individual).

Apple Developer Program: US$99/year, with local currency shown during enrolment. This paid route is outside the free-only publishing work. The existing Mac Test needs a separate sandboxed production build or a TestFlight route. [Apple enrolment](https://developer.apple.com/programs/enroll/).

## Reach people in a useful way

Start with SourceForge and AlternativeTo for relevant app discovery, then share one accurate demonstration through your own social accounts. For Show HN, link something people can actually run and be available for technical questions. Keep feedback links visible and describe Test/signing limits. Do not ask for votes or promise download numbers.
