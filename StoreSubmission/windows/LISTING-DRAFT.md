SketchGlide is a personal drawing project made by Karim Eldamaty. Draw with ten adjustable brushes and ten backgrounds, place searchable maths and engineering symbols, use geometric guides and millimetre dimensions, and keep editable local drawings. Review local OCR and single-symbol recognition, add editable text, use maths tools and explore simple 3D sketch planes and primitives.

Choose light or dark appearance with translucent floating controls. Copy and paste, import images, and export PNG, JPEG or PDF. Windows PDF export uses a raster drawing.
